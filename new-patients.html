New patients

Your first visit

What to expect, what to bring, and how booking, parking and insurance work.

New patients at Optimum Physiotherapy in Mississauga can book directly online or by calling (647) 707-3640 — no doctor's referral is needed for physiotherapy in Ontario. Your first appointment includes a full assessment and hands-on treatment in the same visit, at Suite 108, 1300 Central Parkway West, where there is parking on site.

How do I book my first appointment?

Book online at any hour, or call (647) 707-3640 during opening hours and speak to someone at the clinic. If you are unsure whether to book physiotherapy or massage therapy, call and describe the problem — we will point you to the right one.

What should I wear and bring?

  • Clothing you can move in, and that lets us see the area being treated
  • Any scans, specialist letters or reports you already have
  • Your insurance details, if you intend to claim
