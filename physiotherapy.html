Physiotherapy in Mississauga

Assessment, hands-on treatment and exercise rehabilitation at Suite 108, 1300 Central Parkway West.

Physiotherapy at Optimum Physiotherapy in Mississauga begins with a one-to-one assessment to work out what is actually causing your pain, followed by hands-on treatment and an exercise plan you can keep up with. The clinic treats back, neck, shoulder, knee and hip pain, sports injuries and post-surgical recovery, at Suite 108, 1300 Central Parkway West.

What happens at a first physiotherapy appointment?

Your first appointment is mostly listening and assessing. We ask what happened, what makes it better or worse, and what you are trying to get back to — then examine how you move. Treatment begins in the same visit, so you leave having actually been treated, not just assessed.

You will also leave knowing roughly what to expect: what is likely going on, what we can do about it, and how long it should take. [CONFIRM: typical first appointment length]

What does physiotherapy treatment involve?

Treatment is chosen to suit the problem, not applied from a template.

  • Manual therapy — hands-on work on joints and soft tissue to restore movement and ease pain.
  • Exercise rehabilitation — a small number of exercises that matter, rather than a long list you will not do.
  • Education and pacing — what to do, what to avoid, and how to load the injury safely as it settles.
  • Dry needling — used where it is appropriate. [CONFIRM: is dry needling offered, and by which practitioners]

Do I need a doctor's referral to see a physiotherapist in Ontario?

No. In Ontario you can book physiotherapy directly without a referral from a doctor. Some extended health insurance plans ask for one before they will reimburse you, so it is worth checking your own policy before your first visit.

How many appointments will I need?

It depends on the problem, how long it has been going on, and what you need to get back to. We will give you an honest estimate at your first visit and reassess as you go — and we will tell you when you no longer need us. Physiotherapy outcomes vary between people, and no clinic can promise a specific result.
