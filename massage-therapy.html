Registered Massage Therapy

Therapeutic massage for muscular pain, tension and recovery — on its own or alongside physiotherapy.

Registered massage therapy at Optimum Physiotherapy in Mississauga treats muscular pain, tension and stiffness, and supports recovery from injury and training. It can be booked on its own or alongside a course of physiotherapy, at Suite 108, 1300 Central Parkway West. Appointments are available weekday evenings until 7pm and Saturday mornings.

What is registered massage therapy?

Registered massage therapy is hands-on treatment of muscle and soft tissue by a therapist registered with the College of Massage Therapists of Ontario. It is used for muscular pain and tension, recovery after exertion, and to support rehabilitation from an injury.

Should I book massage therapy or physiotherapy?

If you have an injury, a recent flare-up, or you want to know why something hurts, start with physiotherapy — it begins with an assessment and a diagnosis. If you are dealing with muscular tension, tightness or general recovery, massage therapy is often the better first booking.

If you are not sure, call (647) 707-3640 and describe what is going on. It is a two-minute conversation and it will save you booking the wrong thing.

Can I claim massage therapy on my insurance?

Most extended health plans in Ontario include some coverage for registered massage therapy, though the amount and the conditions vary a great deal between policies. Check your own plan for your annual limit and whether a doctor's note is required.
