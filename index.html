Optimum Physiotherapy is a physiotherapy and registered massage therapy clinic in Mississauga, Ontario, at Suite 108, 1300 Central Parkway West in the Erindale Corporate Centre. The clinic treats back, neck, shoulder, knee and hip pain, sports injuries and post-surgical recovery. Appointments run weekday evenings until 7pm and Saturday mornings, and can be booked online or by phone on (647) 707-3640.

What we do

Two services, done properly

Assessment first, hands-on treatment second, and a plan you can keep up with between visits.

Physiotherapy

A full assessment of what happened and what hurts, manual therapy, and exercise rehabilitation built around your goals rather than a template.
