Conditions

What we treat

The problems patients most often bring to the clinic — and what to expect if you book about one of them.

Optimum Physiotherapy in Mississauga treats musculoskeletal pain and injury — back and neck pain, shoulder, knee and hip problems, sports injuries, post-surgical rehabilitation and repetitive strain. Assessment and treatment happen in the same first appointment at Suite 108, 1300 Central Parkway West. If we are not the right people to help, we will say so.

Back pain

Lower and upper back pain, stiffness and spasm.

Neck pain

Including pain that builds over a day at a desk.

Shoulder injuries

Rotator cuff problems, frozen shoulder, impingement.

Knee and hip pain

Arthritis, overuse, and pain going up or down stairs.

Sports injuries

Sprains, strains and getting safely back to sport.

Post-surgical rehab

Structured recovery after an operation.

Headaches from the neck

Tension headaches that start at the base of the skull.

Work and repetitive strain

Wrist, elbow and shoulder strain from repeated tasks.

This list is not exhaustive and it is not a diagnosis. If your problem is not here, call (647) 707-3640 and ask. [CONFIRM: the full condition list with the clinical team]
