About

A clinic on Central Parkway West

Physiotherapy and registered massage therapy in the Erindale Corporate Centre, Mississauga.

Optimum Physiotherapy is an independent physiotherapy and registered massage therapy clinic in Mississauga, Ontario, at Suite 108, 1300 Central Parkway West in the Erindale Corporate Centre. The clinic offers one-to-one appointments with assessment and hands-on treatment, weekday evenings until 7pm and Saturday mornings, with parking on site.

How we work

One patient at a time, with an assessment before treatment and a plan you understand. We would rather tell you honestly that something will take six weeks than sell you a package you do not need.
