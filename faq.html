FAQ

Questions before you book

Referrals, appointment length, insurance, parking and hours — answered plainly.

Optimum Physiotherapy in Mississauga answers the questions patients ask most before a first appointment: no referral is needed for physiotherapy in Ontario, assessment and treatment happen in the same visit, and the clinic is open until 7pm on weekdays and 9am to 2pm on Saturdays at Suite 108, 1300 Central Parkway West.

Do I need a referral to see a physiotherapist?

No. In Ontario you can book physiotherapy directly without a doctor's referral. Some extended health insurance plans ask for one before they will reimburse you, so check your own policy first.

How long is a first appointment?

Your first visit includes a full assessment and hands-on treatment in the same session. [CONFIRM: exact appointment length]

What should I wear?

Something you can move comfortably in, and that allows access to the area being treated. Shorts are useful for knee and hip problems.

Are you open evenings or weekends?

Yes. The clinic is open until 7pm Monday to Friday, with a 1pm start on Wednesday, and 9am to 2pm on Saturday. Closed Sunday.

Can I book massage therapy without physiotherapy?

Yes. Registered massage therapy can be booked on its own, or alongside a course of physiotherapy.

How many sessions will I need?

That depends on the problem and how long it has been going on. You will get an honest estimate at your first visit and a reassessment as you go. Outcomes vary between people and no clinic can promise a specific result.
